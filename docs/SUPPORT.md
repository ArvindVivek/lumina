# Lumina Support

Need help? Send us a message through [our contact page](https://kitchenlabs-one.vercel.app/contact). We usually reply within two days.

## Common questions

**What matches are in Lumina?**
32 series from three VCT Americas playoff brackets: Stage 1 2024, Stage 1 2025 and Stage 2 2025.
**About the data** (at the bottom of every page) lists them all. The data doesn't update; it's a
fixed sample for study.

**Where do I start?**
Open **Match report** to see one team's side of a series: opening duels, trades, planted rounds
and the rounds worth rewatching. **Player insights** and **Team review** cover one player or one
team across every match.

**What do "traded", "opening duel" and "clutch" mean?**
A death is *traded* when a teammate kills the killer within 5 seconds. An *opening duel* is the
first fight of a round. A *clutch* is when one player is the last one alive on their team and
enemies are still standing.

**What counts as an eco, a force buy and a full buy?**
Lumina uses what the whole team spent when the buy phase ended: under 10,000 credits is an eco,
10,000 to 20,000 a force buy, and 20,000 or more a full buy. Rounds 1 and 13 are pistol rounds.

**Why does the coach say "AI unavailable: showing the numbers"?**
The AI service didn't answer (it may be busy or paused), so Lumina shows the numbers behind your
question instead. Try again in a few minutes.

**Why can't I ask more questions?**
Each visitor can get 20 AI answers an hour, so the coach stays free for everyone. Every page and
number still works without it.

**Why are there no team logos, agent pictures or map images?**
Team logos belong to the teams, so Lumina uses short text badges such as C9 and SEN. Agents and
maps are shown by name.

**Why is there no damage or spike site information?**
The match feed Lumina is built from doesn't include them, so Lumina doesn't guess.
